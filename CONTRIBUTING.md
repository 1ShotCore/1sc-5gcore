# Contributing to 1sc-5gcore

Thanks for helping build an open 5G core. Bug reports, fixes, docs, and test cases are all welcome.

## Sign the CLA first

Before we can merge your first pull request, we need a signed **Contributor License Agreement (CLA)** on file. You sign it once, and it covers all your future contributions.

| You are contributing… | Sign this |
|---|---|
| As yourself, on your own time | [Individual CLA](legal/cla/1sc-icla.pdf) |
| As part of your job, or your employer owns your work | [Corporate CLA](legal/cla/1sc-cla-corporate.pdf), signed by someone authorized at your company, with you listed on Schedule A |

**How to sign**

1. Download the PDF and fill it in. Any PDF form filler works, and an electronic signature is fine.
2. Put your **GitHub username** on the form (Corporate CLA: on Schedule A).
3. Email the signed PDF to **[js@l9.fyi](mailto:js@l9.fyi)**.
4. We add your username to [`.github/CLA_SIGNERS`](.github/CLA_SIGNERS), and the **CLA** check on your PR passes.

If your company signs the Corporate CLA, have its point of contact email us whenever employees join or leave Schedule A.

### Why a CLA?

The core is licensed under **AGPL-3.0**. The CLA lets L9 Technologies LLC also offer the core under a commercial license, for companies that can't use AGPL code. You keep the copyright to your work. The CLA gives us a license to use it, plus a patent license, and it doesn't limit what you do with your own code. Questions: [js@l9.fyi](mailto:js@l9.fyi).

## Pull requests

- Keep each PR to one change and explain *why* in the description.
- Open an issue first for large changes, so we can agree on the approach.
- Don't include code you didn't write without marking its source and license (see section 7 of the CLA).
